A few kind requests

  • By invitation and confirmed RSVP only. Only invited adults may attend. A spouse may be included only when personally invited.
  • Adults only: no children or infants.
  • No pets or other animals. Trained assistance animals can be discussed privately with the organizers where relevant.

  • Respectful conduct: Please help us maintain a peaceful, considerate atmosphere and follow the guidance of the ushers and organizers.
  • Changes: If you can no longer attend after confirming, please contact the organizer promptly so the place can be offered to another guest.
