A note from the wedding organizing team

As Ackrad and Kaluya begin this new chapter together, we warmly invite family and friends who wish to support them to consider a monetary gift rather than household presents, which would be difficult for the couple to transport while living abroad.

For guests who choose to give a monetary gift, the wedding organizing team kindly suggests a minimum contribution of K500. Those who wish to give more are warmly welcome to do so. Gifts are voluntary and are never a requirement for attending; your presence, love and good wishes are deeply valued.

With warm regards, the wedding organizing team. Please only use gift details provided directly by the organizers; do not send money to unverified accounts or numbers.

A few kind requests

  • By invitation and confirmed RSVP only. Registration is for the named invited adult only. Please do not bring additional guests.
  • Adults only: no children or infants.
  • No pets or other animals. Trained assistance animals can be discussed privately with the organizers where relevant.
  • Smoking and vaping are prohibited at our wedding events.
  • No alcohol before 6:00 PM. Guests are kindly asked to respect this arrangement and venue rules throughout the day.
  • Respectful conduct: Please help us maintain a peaceful, considerate atmosphere and follow the guidance of the ushers and organizers.
  • Changes: If you can no longer attend after confirming, please contact the organizer promptly so the place can be offered to another guest.
Our venue and catering arrangements have been made for 150 guests across both families. Unfortunately, this means we cannot accommodate everyone who wishes to join us. We sincerely appreciate your understanding and hope nobody feels left out or sidelined by this limitation.
